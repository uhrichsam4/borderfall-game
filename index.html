        Field manual

        LandClick open land at the start to drop your capital.
        ExpandClick any land you don't own. The attack force slider sets what share of your troops goes. Sea targets launch a transport.
        BuildClick your own land, or pick from the bar (keys 1–7). Cities raise troop cap, factories make gold, ports run trade ships.
        DefendDefense posts triple capture cost nearby. SAM sites shoot down incoming missiles.
        ArmiesReal countries start with their real estimated active-duty troops (China about 2 million, the US 1.3 million, the UK 144,000). Your troop cap comes from the population you control, plus cities and housing.
        TerrainPlains are fastest. Forest, jungle, highlands, desert, tundra and especially mountains slow an advance, cost more troops and favour the defender. Hover any land to see its terrain, temperature and population.
        ClimateTroops in freezing or desert land die of frostbite or heatstroke unless a City or Housing nearby shelters them. Press K for the climate view showing danger zones and your shelter.
        StackingBuild on top of one of your own buildings to stack it: each level adds its full effect. Hold Shift and drag across your land to paint buildings; hold still to keep stacking.
        RailwaysCities, ports and factories link up by rail automatically. Every train that reaches a City or Port pays $2,000 ($1,000 at a factory). Enemies cut lines by taking the land under them.
        NationsMany real countries have traits from their history, like the United Kingdom's Royal Navy, Spain's Armada and Conquistadors, Russia's General Winter and Germany's Blitzkrieg. Hover a country to see it.
        StrikeSilos launch atom (N), hydrogen (H), MIRV (V) and Tsar Bomba (T) missiles. Bigger warheads cost far more and reload slower. Factories unlock airstrikes (A) that carve holes in enemy lines.
        DiplomacyRight-click a nation to propose or break an alliance, or send troops to allies. Breaking one marks you a traitor.
        HuntWhen 20 or fewer rivals remain (or after you continue past victory) a Rivals panel, map markers and edge arrows appear. Click a name or press F to fly to the next one.
        CameraDrag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, Space to recenter. Esc cancels, Q/E nudges the force slider, P pauses.
        WinHold 80% of the land, or be the last nation standing.
